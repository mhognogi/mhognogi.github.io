OnCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

OnCue is a rehearsal partner for actors. It reads the lines of the other characters of a play and listens to your lines. This policy explains what OnCue does with your data.

Short version

OnCue does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Microphone

  • OnCue uses the microphone only during a rehearsal, to know when you have said your line.
  • The speech is turned into text on your device. The audio and the text are not saved and are not sent to any server.

Plays

  • The plays you import, the voices made for them and your settings are saved only on your device, in the storage of the app.
  • If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Purchases

  • The OnCue Pro subscription is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. OnCue receives only the information that the subscription is active.
  • OnCue does not see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Analytics and advertising

OnCue has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Children

OnCue does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
