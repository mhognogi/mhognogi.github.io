Prepare a play file

OnCue reads plays in English. The file can be a text file (.txt), a Word file (.docx), a PDF file (.pdf) or a Final Draft file (.fdx).

Format of a .txt or .docx file

  • Write the title of the play on the first line.
  • Write CHARACTERS: on a line. Under it, write one character on each line: the name in capital letters, a comma and a short description.
  • Write each line of dialogue as the name in capital letters, a colon and the text.
  • Write stage directions in parentheses. OnCue does not say them.
  • To add a pause, write a stage direction with the number of seconds.

Example:

THE LAST TRAIN

CHARACTERS:
ANA, a woman of about 30
VOICE, the voice from the station loudspeaker

(An empty station, at night.)

ANA (to the loudspeaker): Good evening. Is there another train tonight?
VOICE: There is one more, Ana. The last one.
(Pause 5 seconds.)
ANA: No. This time I stay.

Pauses

  • (Pause 5 seconds.) makes a pause of 5 seconds before the next line.
  • (Pause.) makes a pause of 1 second.
  • (Beat.) makes a pause of 2.5 seconds.
  • Put the pause before a line, at the start of a line or at the end of the line before it.

PDF files

  • Use the same format as for a .txt or .docx file: the name, a colon and the text.
  • OnCue does not say lines that are fully in italics. It reads them as stage directions.
  • The list of characters can have the title CHARACTER LIST:. In the list, write a name, a dash and a short description. For example: Ana – a woman of about 30.
  • OnCue cannot read a scanned PDF. A scanned PDF contains pictures of the pages, not text.

Final Draft files

OnCue reads the character names, the dialogue, the parentheticals and the action of a Final Draft file (.fdx). You do not have to change the file.

Add a play

Before you start:

  • You need OnCue Pro to add your plays. Without OnCue Pro, you can rehearse the sample plays.
  • Put the play file in the Files app of your iPad or iPhone. For example, use AirDrop or iCloud Drive.

Copy a play from a Mac with a cable:

  1. Connect the iPad or iPhone to the Mac with a cable.
  2. On the Mac, open Finder. In the sidebar, click your iPad or iPhone.
  3. Click Files.
  4. Drag the play file onto OnCue.

Add the play:

  1. Open OnCue.
  2. Tap Add a play. The Files window opens.
  3. Find the play file and tap it.
    OnCue shows "Reading the play...". Then the play is at the top of the list.

If an error occurs:

  • "Import failed" and "No lines found. Check the format of the play." OnCue found no lines of dialogue. Check that each line starts with the name in capital letters and a colon. See Prepare a play file.
  • "Unknown words": the voice cannot say these words, and it skips them. This happens with some names and rare words. To change this, write the word with a more common spelling in the play file. Then import the play again.

Rehearse a role

Before you start:

  • Allow OnCue to use the microphone. iPadOS and iOS ask you the first time.
  • Rehearse in a quiet room.

Steps:

  1. In the list of plays, tap the play.
  2. Under You play, choose your character.
    Your lines have a gray background.
  3. Optional: under the name of each other character, choose a voice for that character.
  4. Optional: in the last field of the first row, choose the pause before OnCue speaks. The default is 0 seconds. The maximum is 2 seconds.
  5. Tap Start.
    The line that is said now has a stronger gray background.
  6. When OnCue shows "Listening to you...", say your line.
    OnCue shows "Heard:" and the text it heard. When you finish the line, OnCue continues.
  7. To stop for a short time, tap Pause. To continue, tap Resume.
  8. To say a line again, tap the play button (▶) on the right of the line. To go to another line, tap its play button.
    OnCue stops and starts again from that line.
  9. To stop the rehearsal, tap Stop.
    At the end of the play, OnCue shows "End of the play.".

The first rehearsal of a play can start more slowly. OnCue shows "Preparing the voice..." while it makes the voices. The next rehearsals start faster.

If OnCue does not hear you

The bar at the top of the screen shows the volume of the microphone. When you speak, the bar moves.

  • The bar does not move. OnCue cannot use the microphone. Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone. Turn on OnCue. Then close OnCue and open it again.
  • "Microphone error" and "OnCue has no permission to use the microphone." Do the same steps as above.
  • The bar moves only a little. Move the sensitivity slider next to the bar to the right. Then say your line again.
  • OnCue does not continue after your line. Say the last words of the line clearly. OnCue continues when it hears the end of the line.

OnCue Pro subscription

OnCue Pro lets you add your plays and rehearse any role. Without OnCue Pro, you can rehearse the sample plays.

Start OnCue Pro

  1. Tap Add a play. The window "Add your own plays" opens.
  2. Read the price and the conditions.
  3. Tap Try 7 days free. If you already had the free days, the button is Subscribe.
  4. Confirm the purchase in the App Store window.
    The lock goes away, and the Files window opens. Choose your play.

New subscribers get the first 7 days free. Then the subscription renews every month until you cancel it.

Use OnCue Pro on a new device

  1. Sign in with the same Apple Account that you used to buy OnCue Pro.
  2. Open OnCue and tap Add a play.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
    OnCue shows "OnCue Pro is active.".

If OnCue shows "No active OnCue Pro subscription was found for this Apple Account.", check that you use the correct Apple Account.

Cancel OnCue Pro

In OnCue, tap the help button (the question mark), then Manage subscription. Or use Settings:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap OnCue Pro, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. After the period ends, your plays stay in OnCue, but you can rehearse only the sample plays.
